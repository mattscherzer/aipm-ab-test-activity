Recommendation

3 / 3
Roll out

Every plausible value of the effect clears our bar for shipping, and no guardrail moved the wrong way. Readers who clicked also scrolled further, stayed longer and bounced less — more clicks and better ones, not a trade.

Risks we are carrying

  • Fewer headlines above the fold. Summaries take space — readers saw 4.3% fewer headlines per visit. Ad inventory per visit was not instrumented, so the revenue side of that is unmeasured.
  • Subscriber cancellations were never tested. The data we have contains no cancellation events, so that guardrail is untested rather than passed.
  • Only 87% of stories had a summary written. The measured effect is therefore a floor, not a ceiling.

Next steps, in order

  • 1 Instrument ad inventory per visit before the rollout is permanent. This is the one open question that could change the answer.
  • 2 Run the cancellation check against real subscriber data, and keep watching the exposed cohorts for 60–90 days.
  • 3 Push summary coverage past 87%. Coverage multiplies straight through to the effect — this is the cheapest remaining upside.
  • 4 Test summary length next — one sentence against two. The lever works; now tune it.
